3. MODEL TRAINING ISSUES & ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During execution of `python run_all_notebooks.py`, the training loop for Phase 1 
(01_image_efficientnet_b4.ipynb) was interrupted after ~3 hours.

Root Cause Analysis:
- Dataset Scale vs Compute Resource:
  The notebook dataset loader processed the full un-capped dataset (19,200 images) 
  at 380x380 resolution. Running EfficientNet-B4 fine-tuning for 3 epochs over 
  19,200 images on CPU (Intel i3-12100F) requires ~600 batches/epoch, taking 
  ~1.5 to 2 hours per epoch.
- Output Buffering:
  `run_all_notebooks.py` captures cell stdout in memory buffers (io.StringIO), 
  preventing live batch progress lines from rendering in the console until the 
  entire cell execution finishes. This gave the appearance of a frozen terminal.
